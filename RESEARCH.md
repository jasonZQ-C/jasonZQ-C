## 01 / Computer Vision · 计算机视觉

**How can a visual model remain reliable beyond the categories and conditions it has seen?**

关注视觉表征的迁移与可靠性，尤其是工业视觉中的异常检测、缺陷定位，以及少量正常参考样本条件下的检测能力。

Topics of interest:

- Visual representation learning / 视觉表征学习
- Visual anomaly detection and localization / 视觉异常检测与定位
- Few-shot normal-reference adaptation / 少量正常参考样本适配
- Cross-category generalization / 跨类别泛化

## 02 / Embodied Intelligence · 具身智能

**How can visual understanding be grounded in space, action, and interaction?**

对连接视觉感知、空间推理和行动学习感兴趣，希望进一步理解视觉表征如何服务于与真实环境交互的智能体。

Topics I would like to explore:

- Spatially grounded visual representations / 具备空间理解的视觉表征
- Perception–action learning / 感知与行动学习
- Multimodal perception and reasoning / 多模态感知与推理
- Generalization in embodied environments / 具身环境中的泛化

## 03 / Research practice · 科研实践

I value clear problem definitions, reproducible pipelines, and careful evaluation. A working method should be accompanied by explicit data protocols, meaningful comparisons, and an honest account of its limitations.

重视问题定义、数据划分和实验可复现性；希望在性能之外，也关注泛化、计算成本和方法的适用边界。通过清晰的对比与消融来理解结果，而不是只追求单一指标。
