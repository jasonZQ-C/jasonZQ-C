# Research Interests · 研究兴趣

## Computer Vision · 计算机视觉

I am interested in visual representations that remain useful across objects and categories, with a focus on industrial anomaly detection, defect localization, and adaptation using limited normal references.

关注能够跨物体、跨类别迁移的视觉表征，重点探索工业异常检测、缺陷定位与少量正常参考样本下的适配。

## Embodied Intelligence · 具身智能

I am interested in connecting visual perception with spatial understanding and interaction, and in studying how perception can support action in physical environments.

关注视觉感知与空间理解、环境交互的结合，探索感知如何为真实环境中的行动提供支持。

## Research Practice · 科研实践

I value clear experimental protocols, reproducibility, and careful analysis of generalization. These notes describe research interests; publications and validated results will be listed separately when available.

重视清晰的实验协议、可复现性与对泛化能力的认真分析。本页描述研究兴趣，论文与经过验证的研究结果将在信息完善后单独列出。
